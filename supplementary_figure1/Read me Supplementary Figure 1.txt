Read me: Supplementary Figure 1 – Voltage Trace Comparison Between Models

Description:
This folder contains the code required to reproduce Supplementary Figure 1.

The figure compares membrane voltage traces generated by:
- Original Hodgkin–Huxley (HH) model
- Modified HH model

The comparison is performed under different applied current values (I_app),
showing the correspondence between the two models in spike shape and timing.

Main Script:
- supplementary_figure1.m
  → Generates the full figure

Computation Notes:
- The script contains full implementations of both the original HH model and the modified model.
- Simulations are performed directly within the script.
- Runtime is short (a few seconds).

Data Handling:
- No external data files are required.
- All computations are performed within the script.

Output:
- The script generates Supplementary Figure 1 as shown in the manuscript.

Requirements:
- No additional toolboxes required

Alt text:
Three panels of membrane-potential traces over 200 ms from the original Hodgkin-Huxley model (orange) and the Hill-based HH model (navy). In each panel, a black bar marks the 100-ms current step applied from 50 to 150 ms. The applied-current densities for the original and Hill-based models are 30 and 33 µA/cm² in panel a, 50 and 57 µA/cm² in panel b, and 70 and 80 µA/cm² in panel c. Both models rest near −65 mV, fire repetitively during the step, and return to rest afterwards. The two traces almost overlap, showing closely matched spike timing and broadly similar waveforms; the navy spike peaks are slightly higher, and there are small differences in afterhyperpolarization.
