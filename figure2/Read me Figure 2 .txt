Read me: Figure 2 – Comparison of Applied Current Mapping and Firing Response

Description:
This folder contains the code required to reproduce Figure 2.

- Panel a: Linear relationship between the applied current in the original HH model and the modified model (I_app mapping with RMSE < 10).
- Panel b: Firing frequency versus applied current (I_app) for both the original Hodgkin–Huxley (HH) model and the modified model.

Main Script:

  → Generates the full figure (Panels A and B)

- Panel b:
  The script contains the full implementation of both the original HH model and the modified model.

Alt Text:
Two panels comparing the original Hodgkin-Huxley (HH) model (orange) and the
modified HH model (navy). Panel a shows the applied current in the modified HH
model plotted against the applied current in the original HH model, from 
10 to 120 µA/cm². Orange markers show current pairs that produce matched
responses (RMSE < 10 mV), and they fall on the navy best linear fit,
I_mod = 1.178 × I_HH − 2.041, with a coefficient of determination almost equal
to 1. Panel b shows the frequency-current (f–I) relationships of both models
from 0 to 200 µA/cm². The modified model closely reproduces the behavior of the
original model, including firing onset, the stepwise increase in firing rate
over intermediate inputs, the high-frequency plateau at about 160 Hz, and the
breakdown of sustained repetitive firing at strong drive. The modified curve is
shifted slightly to higher currents: for example, firing breaks down at about
145 µA/cm² instead of about 125 µA/cm².
