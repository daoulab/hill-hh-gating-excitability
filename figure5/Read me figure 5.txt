Read me: Figure 5 – Mutation Mapping and Sensitivity to Kinetic Assumptions

Description:
This folder contains the code and data required to reproduce Figure 5.

The figure includes:
- Panel a: Schematic of the mapping from reported steady-state gating
  descriptors to model implementations.
- Panel b: A863P-to-wild-type (WT) rheobase ratios across Hill-based and
  classical Hodgkin–Huxley (HH) implementations, compared with the
  experimental mutant-to-WT current-threshold ratio (dashed line).
- Panel c: Classical HH rheobase ratios after multiplying the activation rates
  by phi_m and the inactivation rates by phi_h (kinetic-rescaling grid).
- Panel d: Experimental mutant-to-WT current-threshold ratios compared with
  Hill-model rheobase ratios for A863P, F1449V and A1632G.

Main Script:
- figure5.m
  → Generates the full figure (panels a–d) by calling the panel functions below

Panel Functions (called by figure5.m):
- panelA_schematic_draw.m → draws panel a
- Figure_PanelB.m         → draws panel b
- Figure_PanelC.m         → draws panel c
- Figure_PanelD.m         → draws panel d

Data Files (in this folder):
- Input data (loaded by the panel c function):
    - Nav17_HH_tau_rescaling_results.mat

- Saved by the script (created each time it runs):
  - PanelB_data.mat → values plotted in panel b
  - PanelC_data.mat → values plotted in panel c
  - PanelD_data.mat → values plotted in panel d
    (mutations, experimental and predicted WT/mutant values, and ratios)

Computation Notes:
- No simulations are performed in this script.
- The script loads the saved data and generates the figure.
- Runtime is very fast (a few seconds).

Output:
- The script generates figure5.pdf, the same as Figure 5 in the manuscript.
- It also saves figure5.png.

Requirements:
- No additional toolboxes required

Alt Text:
Four panels about mutation mapping and sensitivity to kinetic assumptions.
Panel a is a Schematic of the mapping from reported steady-state gating descriptors 
to model implementations. The Hill-based mapping retains specified complementary 
rates and an assumed inactivation construction; steady-state descriptors alone do 
not determine gating kinetics in either formulation. Panel b shows 
A863P-to-wild-type (WT) rheobase ratios for three Hill-HH and six classical HH
implementations, with a dashed line at the experimental ratio of about 0.63.
The Hill-HH values cluster tightly near 0.4, while the classical HH values
spread from about 0.15 to 1 depending on the implementation. Panel c is a heat
map of classical HH rheobase ratios after multiplying the activation rates by
phi_m and the inactivation rates by phi_h (each from 0.25 to 4). The ratio
ranges from 0.21 to 0.99 and rises as phi_h increases or phi_m decreases; the
outlined unscaled reference cell (phi_m = phi_h = 1) is 0.41. Panel d compares
experimental mutant-to-WT current-threshold ratios with Hill-HH predicted
rheobase ratios: 0.63 vs 0.41 for A863P, 0.75 vs 0.46 for F1449V, and 0.65 vs
0.36 for A1632G. The Hill-based predictions reproduce the direction of the
experimental changes but overestimate their magnitude.
