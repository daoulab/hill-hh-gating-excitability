Read me: Supplementary Figure 6 – Variability in A863P Rheobase Predictions Across Accepted Fitting Ensembles

Description:
This folder contains the code and data required to reproduce Supplementary
Figure 6, which shows the mutant-to-wild-type (WT) rheobase ratios for the
200 accepted fits in each implementation:

- Panel a: Classical Hodgkin–Huxley (HH) ensemble with all sodium-gating rate
  parameters free (orange)
- Panel b: Hill-based HH ensemble with the amplitude and voltage scale of the
  alpha_h inactivation rate free (navy)

Each point is one accepted fit; the horizontal axis indexes fits and does not
denote a parameter sweep. The solid colored line is the ensemble median, and
the dashed gray line is the experimental A863P/WT current-threshold ratio.

Main Script:
- Supplementary_Figure_6.m
  → Generates the full figure (panels a and b)

Data Files (in this folder):
- Nav17_HH_starts_ensemble_boltz.mat → classical HH ensemble (panel a)
- Nav17_HillAH_results.mat           → Hill-based HH ensemble (panel b)

These files contain precomputed fitting results.
Implementation details are provided in Supplementary Table S7.

Papers:
- Located in the "papers" folder:
  - Harty2006_A863P.pdf, the source of the experimental A863P ratio and the
    published steady-state targets
  - references.txt → full reference and DOI

Computation Notes:
- No fitting or simulations are performed in this script.
- The script loads the saved ensembles and draws the figure.
- Runtime is very fast (a few seconds).

Output:
- The script generates Supplementary_Figure_6.pdf and Supplementary_Figure_6.png
  , the same as Supplementary Figure 6 in the manuscript.

Requirements:
- No additional toolboxes required

Alt Text (for the journal):
Two scatter plots of A863P-to-wild-type (WT) rheobase ratios for 200 accepted
fits in each implementation, plotted against accepted fit index; the
horizontal axis indexes fits and does not denote a parameter sweep. Panel a
shows the classical Hodgkin-Huxley (HH) ensemble with all sodium-gating rate
parameters free (orange), and panel b shows the Hill-based HH ensemble with the
amplitude and voltage scale of alpha_h rate free (navy).
Both panels use the same vertical scale. In each panel, a solid colored line
marks the ensemble median and a dashed gray line marks the experimental
A863P/WT current-threshold ratio of about 0.63. The classical HH ratios are
widely scattered, from about 0.15 to 0.93, with a median of 0.298; most fall
below the experimental ratio, but some lie above it. The Hill-based ratios
form a narrow band from 0.346 to 0.446, with a median of 0.395, all below the
experimental ratio. Because the two ensembles use different targets and rate
assumptions, the narrower Hill-based distribution reflects repeatability under
its specified targets rather than uniquely determined kinetics or superior
quantitative prediction.
