Read me: Supplementary Figure 4 – Sodium-Gating Time Constants Across A863P Fitting Ensembles

Description:
This folder contains the code and data required to reproduce Supplementary
Figure 4, which shows the voltage dependence of the sodium activation time
constant (tau_m) and inactivation time constant (tau_h) for wild type (WT)
and A863P in:

- Classical Hodgkin–Huxley (HH) implementation (panels a–d, orange)
- Hill-based HH implementation (panels e–h, navy)

The figure includes:
- Panels a, e: tau_m, WT
- Panels b, f: tau_m, A863P
- Panels c, g: tau_h, WT
- Panels d, h: tau_h, A863P

In each panel, thin lines show individual accepted fits, the thick line is the
ensemble median, and the dashed gray line is the voltage-shifted classical HH
time constant used as a reference.

Main Script:
- Supplementary_Figure_4.m
  → Generates the full figure (panels a–h)

Data Files (in this folder):
- tau.mat → precomputed fitting ensembles (time constants and steady-state
  curves of all accepted fits, the published target curves, and the
  classical HH reference)

Papers:
- Located in the "papers" folder:
  - Harty2006_A863P.pdf, the source of the published A863P steady-state curves
  - references.txt → full reference and DOI

Computation Notes:
- No fitting or simulations are performed in this script.
- The script loads tau.mat, computes the shifted classical HH reference
  curves from the classical rate equations, and draws the figure.
- Time constants are plotted on a logarithmic axis.
- Runtime is very fast (a few seconds).

Output:
- The script generates Supplementary_Figure_4.pdf and Supplementary_Figure_4.png,
  the same as Supplementary Figure 4 in the manuscript.

Requirements:
- No additional toolboxes required

Alt Text:
Eight panels showing the voltage dependence of the sodium activation time
constant (τm) and inactivation time constant (τh) across A863P fitting
ensembles, from −120 to 40 mV on a logarithmic time-constant axis. Panels a–d
show the classical Hodgkin-Huxley (HH) ensemble (orange) and panels e–h the
Hill-based HH ensemble (navy): τm for wild type (WT) in panels a and e, τm for
A863P in panels b and f, τh for WT in panels c and g, and τh for A863P in
panels d and h. Thin lines show individual fits, thick lines indicate ensemble
medians, and dashed gray lines show the voltage-shifted classical HH time
constants used as references. In the classical panels, the τm fits form a
narrow band that rises steeply with voltage and peaks between about −10 and
0 mV, while the τh fits spread widely, over about four orders of magnitude. In
the Hill-based panels, most fits lie close together. The Hill-based τm follows
the shifted classical curve up to its peak near −20 mV for WT and lies below it
for A863P, while the Hill-based τh stays lower and flatter than the classical
reference at negative voltages. The ensembles illustrate the kinetic
variability compatible with the selected steady-state targets and rate
assumptions; because the two ensembles use different inactivation targets,
they do not constitute a comparison under identical constraints.
